Clog on Discretion
By M. Qasim Khan Khattak, Advocate

This article examines Section 54-C of the Electricity Act, 1910, and the doctrine of clog on judicial discretion. Courts must not allow unreasonable electricity bills, double jeopardy, or statutory restrictions to permanently clog judicial discretion where interim injunctions, natural justice, and equity require relief. The clog on discretion must be removed in appropriate cases so that justice is not frustrated by rigid application of Section 54-C.
